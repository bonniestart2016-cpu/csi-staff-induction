Induction information / Teaching & research

Academic work

Teaching colleagues should meet the Student Experience Team. Research and finance support is coordinated through central shared services and relevant University offices.

Teaching support

Student Experience supports UG, PGT and PGR matters. Arrange a meeting about module documents, examinations, coursework, attendance, timetables, Canvas and student support.

Examinations

The induction checklist names Dr John Fearnley as Examinations Officer.

Research support

Pre-award proposals, post-award grants, expenses and payments are identified support areas. The shared services hub covers purchasing, travel and finance.

Research development

Research staff should discuss appropriate training with their line manager, including development opportunities and the Prosper programme.

Seminars

The source lists School seminars on Tuesdays, 1–2pm, with Dr Lutz Oettershagen as a contact. Confirm the current schedule.
